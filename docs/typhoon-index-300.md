# Hazard Category: Typhoon Index 300

The Typhoon Index 300 represents an extreme environmental loading scenario.

## Simulation Variables
- **Wind Target**: 300 kph
- **Precipitation**: Horizontal heavy rain
- **Debris**: Light debris including leaves, twigs, and small branches
- **Structural Impacts**:
  - Roof uplift forces
  - Wall pressure and suction forces
  - Frame racking
  - Cladding vibration and detachment risk
